# Cryptid Containment: the Field Manual

The Field Manual is the strategy guide for **Cryptid Containment**, a cozy, deadpan monster-catching RPG. You play a
crash-landed Grey mistaken for the new hire at a government field office. Your job is to contain Bigfoot, the Mothman
and 128 more anomalies while you look for a way home.

- **Read the guide:** https://minormending.github.io/cryptid-containment-guide/
- **Play in the browser:** https://cryptid-containment.pages.dev/
- **Play on Android:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.minormending.cryptidcontainment
  ("Cryptid Containment: Idle RPG")

The guide covers every chapter's walkthrough, every place, Site leader and trainer, all 130 anomalies with their case
files, the hoaxes and their evidence trails, items and the battle rules.

## About this repository

It holds **only the generated site**, served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch. Every table, picture and number in it
is generated from the game's own data by the game's build tools, and the whole site is replaced on each release, so a
hand edit here would be lost. Please do not edit these files by hand or open pull requests against them.

Found a mistake in the guide or the game? Write to goldenrod.works+cryptid@gmail.com.
